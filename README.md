**Solstice Heat** is an original open-world crime game set in **Solstice Bay**, a sun-soaked coastal city of art-deco pastel blocks, glass downtown towers, palm-lined boulevards and a golden-hour ocean. You play **Rafa Solis**, a wheelman framed for the Meridian Casino heist, clearing his name across **seven story jobs** with fixer Tere Solano while Detective Hale turns every SBPD unit against you.

Steal and drive any car, walk the sidewalks, fight the crowd's panic, and outrun a five-star wanted level: pursuing cruisers ram you, roadblocks cut the coast road, and **Air-1** hunts you with a searchlight at three stars and up. Traffic and pedestrians live their own lives; fists, pistol, SMG, rifle and shotgun unlock as the story pays out cash and respect. A dense HUD carries the mission panel, wanted stars, a named-unit SBPD response tracker, a circular minimap, weapon bar, radio, speedometer and condition, health/respect bars and portrait dialogue. Everything renders in low-poly flat-shaded 3D with three.js, every sound is synthesized with WebAudio, and progress saves to localStorage. No network, no build step, no borrowed assets — the whole city is procedural and original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Drive with **W A S D**, **SPACE** handbrake, **F** to enter or steal a car, **SHIFT** to sprint on foot, mouse or **J** to shoot, **1–5** weapons, **R** reload, **Q E** camera, **V** radio, **ESC** pause.
